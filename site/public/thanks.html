Getting your key

Your payment went through. This takes a few seconds.

To use it

  1. Copy the key.
  2. Open Imago Emulator and choose Settings.
  3. Under Supporter, press Paste Key.

The key has also been emailed to the address you gave at the checkout, and your receipt comes from Paddle. The key works offline, on each of your own computers.

If you have paid and your key does not come, write to support@imagoemulator.com with the order number from your receipt.
