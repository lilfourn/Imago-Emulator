Refunds

Last changed on 19 November 2026.

Any supporter key can be refunded in full within 30 days of buying it. You don't have to give a reason.

How to ask

Write to support@imagoemulator.com from the email address you bought with, or include the order number from your receipt. You can also ask at paddle.net, where Paddle, which sold you the key, looks up your order and has a Request refund button.

How it is paid

Paddle sends the money back to the card or account you paid with. We ask Paddle for the refund as soon as we read your message, usually within two working days. How long the money then takes to arrive depends on your bank.

What happens to the key

A refunded key stops working in a later version of the app. The app itself stays free and complete, and you keep your games and saves.

After 30 days

Write to us anyway and say what went wrong. We decide those one at a time.

Your rights

This is in addition to any right the law gives you where you live, such as the 14 days that buyers in the European Union and the United Kingdom have to change their mind. Paddle's refund policy sets those out.
